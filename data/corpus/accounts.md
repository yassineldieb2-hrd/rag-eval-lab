# Account and Security

## Resetting your password
Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email. The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If no email arrives, check the spam folder before requesting another.

## Two-factor authentication
Enable two-factor authentication under Security settings. We support authenticator apps and recovery codes. SMS codes are not supported. Store the ten recovery codes somewhere safe, each works once.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion removes your profile and saved addresses within 14 days. Invoices are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it.

## Changing your email
Go to Profile and select Change email. A confirmation message is sent to the new address and the old address stays active until you confirm.
