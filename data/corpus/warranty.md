# Warranty and Repairs

## Coverage
All devices carry a 24 month warranty against manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, water damage and unauthorised repairs are not covered.

## Starting a repair
Register the serial number in your account, describe the fault and print the prepaid label. Repairs take about 10 business days after the device arrives at our service centre.

## Battery replacement
Batteries are consumables. Capacity below 80 percent within the first 12 months is treated as a defect and replaced free of charge. After 12 months a replacement costs 39 EUR.
