# Subscriptions

## Plans
The Basic plan costs 9 EUR per month and includes one household. The Plus plan costs 19 EUR per month and adds priority support and up to five households.

## Cancelling
You can cancel at any time in Billing settings. Access continues until the end of the paid period and no further charges are made. Cancelling does not trigger a prorated refund.

## Failed payments
When a payment fails we retry three times over seven days and email you each time. After the third failure the subscription is paused, and reactivating it needs a new payment method.

## Invoices
Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing period and can be downloaded as PDF from Billing history. VAT numbers must be added before the invoice is issued, not afterwards.
