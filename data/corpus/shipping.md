# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the EU. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 EUR. Orders placed after 14:00 CET ship the next working day.

## Free shipping
Orders above 60 EUR qualify for free standard shipping inside the EU. Express shipping is never free.

## Tracking a parcel
A tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves our warehouse. If the link shows no movement for 5 days, open a ticket and we will start a carrier investigation.

## International orders
We ship to Norway, Switzerland and the United Kingdom. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.
