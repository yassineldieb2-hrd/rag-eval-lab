# Privacy and Data

## What we store
We store your name, delivery addresses, order history and support tickets. We do not store full card numbers; payments are handled by our payment processor.

## Requesting your data
You can request an export of your personal data from Privacy settings. The export is prepared within 30 days and sent as a ZIP file with JSON files.

## Marketing emails
Marketing emails are opt-in. Every message has an unsubscribe link, and changes take effect within 48 hours.
