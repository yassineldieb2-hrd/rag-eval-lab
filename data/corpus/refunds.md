# Refunds and Returns

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging. Gift cards and downloadable software cannot be returned.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days after we receive the parcel. Bank transfers can take up to 14 days.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an order arrives damaged or you received the wrong product, contact support within 7 days with a photo. We send a replacement at no cost or refund the full price including shipping.
